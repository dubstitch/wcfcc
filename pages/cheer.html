Divisions

AGE GROUP
EXPECTATIONS

Two cheer divisions, same program path. Each group trains at the right pace for their age, confidence, and skill level.

Foundations division. Athletes ages 8–10 build cheer voice, motion placement, beginner jumps, rhythm, and confidence on the sideline.

Skills emphasized: Cheer voice & projection · Motion sharpness · Jump basics · Simple choreography · Formations · Confidence.

Performance division. Athletes ages 11–13 sharpen technique, choreography, timing, jumps, and performance presence.

Skills emphasized: Advanced motion combinations · Jump sequence · Routine execution · Crowd engagement · Synchronization · Showmanship.

The Journey

CHEER
PROGRESSION

Every rep builds toward Championship Day. Athletes progress week by week through cheers, motion technique, the routine, and Friday game day reps.

1
Week 1

CHEER BASICS + FIRST ROUTINE PIECE

Athletes learn camp expectations, basic motions, counts, beginner chants and cheers, and the first piece of the Championship Day routine. By Friday, they begin building confidence by practicing what they learned during game day activities.

  • Basic motions and counts
  • Beginner chants and cheers
  • First routine section
  • Friday game day practice
2
Week 2

MORE CHEERS + ROUTINE BUILDING

Athletes continue building technique while learning additional chants, cheers, and the next section of the Championship Day routine. Coaches focus on timing, sharper motions, confidence, and connecting material from previous weeks.

  • Additional chants and cheers
  • Second routine section
  • Timing and motion clean up
  • Friday game day practice
3
Week 3

FINAL ROUTINE SECTION + FULL RUN THROUGHS

Athletes learn the final section of the Championship Day routine and begin practicing the routine together in fuller run throughs. The focus shifts toward memory, confidence, teamwork, transitions, and stronger overall execution.

  • Final routine section
  • Full routine run throughs
  • Transition and timing practice
  • Friday game day preparation
4
Week 4

ROUTINE CLEAN UP + CHAMPIONSHIP DAY PREP

Athletes spend the final week polishing the full routine and preparing for Championship Day performances. Coaches focus on cleaner motions, sharper timing, stronger confidence, and bringing the team together before the final event.

  • Full routine clean up
  • Performance confidence
  • Timing and synchronization
  • Championship Day preparation
